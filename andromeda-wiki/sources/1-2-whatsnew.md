## Notes

- The **27 January 1998 restructuring** is the most consequential entry: it deliberately removed AGDS-duplicate sections and renumbered the corpus. Several wiki sources pages (notably [[sources/2-2-interpreter]], [[sources/5-2-picture]], [[sources/4-2-logic]]) carry IA-date 27 Jan 1998 and reflect this restructuring.
- The **3 March 1998 `put` / `put.v` correction** is already captured in [[interpreter/commands]] via [[sources/4-3-logic]] — flagged here for traceability.
- This chapter does **not** record corrections to format claims (byte layouts, opcode tables); only structural and textual edits. A claim being unchanged since 31 August 1997 means only that it hasn't been edited, not that it has been validated.
