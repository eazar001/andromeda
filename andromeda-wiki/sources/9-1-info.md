## Scope

Contributor roll. Thirteen hobbyist AGI developers listed with email addresses, personal/project websites, and one-line activity summaries. Bibliographic only.

## Notable contributors already cited in the wiki

- **Lance Ewing** (`be@ihug.co.nz`, *The Hidden Secrets of Sierra*) — primary reverse-engineering source; author of [[sources/4-1-logic]], [[sources/6-3-view]], [[sources/7-1-sound]], [[sources/8-1-otherdata]], [[sources/8-2-words-tok]], [[sources/2-4-interpreter]], [[sources/2-8-interpreter]]. Reference decoders: `showlog`, `showpic`, `play`, `oldplay`, `volx`, `xv3`, `glue`, `picedit`.
- **Vassili Bykov** (`vbykov@CAM.ORG`) — Russian→English translator of the AGDS manual; primary author of [[sources/2-6-interpreter]], [[sources/4-4-logic]], [[sources/5-2-picture]] in the corpus. See [[sources/9-2-agds]] for AGDS provenance.
- **Peter Kelly** (`ptrkelly@ozemail.com.au`, *Sierra Classics*) — corpus compiler and editor; author of *AGI Studio* and predecessor *AGIHack*; author of [[sources/4-2-logic]], [[sources/4-3-logic]], [[sources/6-1-view]], [[sources/8-3-samplecode]].

Other contributors listed but not (yet) cited as sources: Nat Budin, Nuno Costa, Jim Fowler, Jeremy Hayes, Drew Klepper, Kirk Klobe, Joakim Möller, Carl Muckenhoupt, Mike Phillips, Jens Christian Restemeier, Martin Tillenius. Several are credited indirectly through reference-implementation files (e.g., Restemeier on `play.c`, cited in [[sources/7-2-sound]]).
